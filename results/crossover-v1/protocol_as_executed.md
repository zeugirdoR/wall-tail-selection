# Boundary-geometric suite — protocol before calibration and confirmation

Two fixed families: symmetric equal-weight N(±mu,1) mixture, and variance component N(0,1+mu²), versus M0=N(0,1). Both have mu∈[0,2], uniform proper mu prior and equal model priors. The prior in eta=mu² is 1/(4 sqrt(eta)) on [0,4]; numerical integration in mu retains this measure exactly. It is not a uniform-eta prior.

Each family has n=32,128,512 and seven cells per n: local mu=h n^(-1/4), h=0,0.5,1,2; boundary mu=k(log(n)/n)^(1/4), k=0.8,1.2; regular fixed mu=1. Total42 cells. Null h=0 is included in the local region but excluded from alternative-power summaries. All regions and unfavorable cells are reported. No second campaign or outcome-driven tuning.

Candidate: quadratic exact log likelihood in eta around its constrained MLE, retaining the score at either boundary, integrated over the actual prior/domain. Ablation: quadratic in mu around the same MLE, retaining boundary score, same prior/domain. This changes the expansion coordinate and hence the retained higher-order terms in mu; it is not a claim that a unique intrinsic geometric correction has been isolated. Call the candidate boundary-Laplace, not a proven novel TOI method. If curvature is nonnegative/nonfinite, or the integrated approximation is nonfinite or exceeds the exact constrained likelihood peak, use exact evidence, with reason/frequency and costs explicitly recorded for BOTH candidates. No data-dependent switching threshold or tuned constant.

Controls: exact evidence, numerical exact LOO, WAIC, two-model Drton–Plummer sBIC, numerical WBIC at beta=1/log n, likelihood ratio, sum-of-squares score, BIC (weak control), crude RLCT-only and previous quartic local shape (failure control). BMA and LOO stacking are separate aggregation references. All predictive selectors use identical exact within-model posterior predictives. Truth is used only by the evaluation integral, never by inference. WBIC score is E_beta[log likelihood ratio], not divided by beta. sBIC uses lambda10=1/4,lambda11=1/2,multiplicities1 under the smooth positive mu prior, with the positive root and equal model weights; omitted O(1) factors are inherent to this asymptotic proxy.

Variance structural ceiling: under one-sided variance alternatives, S2=sum x² has monotone likelihood ratio; its exact-alpha upper-tail chi-square test is UMP. Exact BF is strictly increasing in S2. A genuine matched-level geometric power improvement over this test is impossible here. The same analytic score test is a valid level-alpha control in the mixture family but not generally UMP there.

Threshold is ascending order statistic ceil((m+1)*0.95), reject only strictly greater; this provides marginal conformal type-I control at alpha0.05 under exchangeability, not a conditional guarantee for each realized threshold. Analytic chi-square score threshold is included separately. Native evidence/LOO/WAIC/sBIC/WBIC/candidate decisions use their fixed zero threshold. LR and moment have no native-zero predictive-selector claim; they enter matched-alpha structure only.
